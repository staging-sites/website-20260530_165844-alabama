## Introduction

Mapping and analysing UFO sightings across Maine reveals how reports of unexplained aerial phenomena are distributed across the state and what patterns emerge from decades of witness accounts. Rather than affirming any particular interpretation of these sightings, this analysis focuses on **where reports cluster, how frequently they occur in different towns, and what broader patterns the data suggest about sightings in Maine**. Key publicly available sources such as the National UFO Reporting Center (NUFORC) and local media compilations allow us to trace both the geographic spread of reports and the broader statistical picture within the state.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://1019therock.com/maine-ufos-reported-sightings-2024/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: 1019therock.com">[101.9 The Rock]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">1019therock.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">maine ufos reported sightings 2024</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">101.9 The RockHere&#x27;s Where the 30 UFO Sightings Were in Maine in 2024December 10, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 10, 2024</span></span></span>

## Geographic Distribution of Reports

Reports of unidentified aerial phenomena in Maine are spread across both **coastal and inland areas**, from sparsely populated rural towns to the state’s busiest cities. In 2024 alone, at least 30 sightings were reported to the NUFORC, occurring in a wide array of locations including **Eastport, Portland, Boothbay, Scarborough, Lisbon, Windham, Prospect, Biddeford, and Waterboro** among others.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://1019therock.com/maine-ufos-reported-sightings-2024/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: 1019therock.com">[101.9 The Rock]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">1019therock.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">maine ufos reported sightings 2024</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">101.9 The RockHere&#x27;s Where the 30 UFO Sightings Were in Maine in 2024December 10, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 10, 2024</span></span></span>

This broad geographic distribution suggests that sightings are not confined to one region of Maine but appear wherever observers are present and notice unusual sky phenomena. The available lists of reported sightings show a mix of small towns and larger population centres; this mixture partly reflects where people live and report observations but also reveals that sightings aren’t limited to any single type of landscape within the state.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://1019therock.com/maine-ufos-reported-sightings-2024/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: 1019therock.com">[101.9 The Rock]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">1019therock.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">maine ufos reported sightings 2024</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">101.9 The RockHere&#x27;s Where the 30 UFO Sightings Were in Maine in 2024December 10, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 10, 2024</span></span></span>

Over longer time periods, compilations of NUFORC data highlight dozens of Maine towns with repeated reports. Although comprehensive official maps of sighting points specific to Maine are not always publicly available, broader geocoded datasets — which plot tens of thousands of sightings across the United States — include state-level clusters that align with these reported towns.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.ufodatalive.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufodatalive.com">[UFO Data Live]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufodatalive.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Source details in endnotes.</span></span></span>

## Patterns and Statistical Analysis

When viewed over long time frames, **Maine shows a relatively high rate of UFO sightings [per capita]({{ 'per-capita/' | relative_url }})** compared with many other US states. National-level analysis of NUFORC and related data suggests that Maine’s observed rate — roughly one report per 1 358 residents — places it above the nationwide average.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://countryherald.com/news/maine-u-s-ufo-rate-at-1-in-1358-residents/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: countryherald.com">[Country Herald]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">countryherald.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Country Herald Maine, U.S. – UFO Rate at 1 in 1,358 Residents</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Country HeraldMaine, U.S. – UFO Rate at 1 in 1,358 Residents - Country HeraldMarch 24, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 24, 2026</span></span></span> Historically, UFO research outlets and compilations have also ranked Maine near the top nationally for sightings per resident, alongside states like Washington, Montana and Vermont in older datasets.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.pressherald.com/2014/03/07/in_maine__we_are_not_alone__ufo_trackers_say_/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: pressherald.com">[The Portland Press Herald]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">pressherald.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Portland Press Herald In Maine, we are not alone, UFO trackers say</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Portland Press HeraldIn Maine, we are not alone, UFO trackers sayMarch 7, 2014...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 7, 2014</span></span></span>

This does not imply that Maine sees more unexplained phenomena than other places, but **it does reflect a combination of clear night skies, community engagement with reporting, and geographic coverage** that results in many submissions. Variables affecting spatial distribution include population centres (where more observers live), levels of light pollution, and seasonal activity, although detailed statistical work specific to Maine remains limited outside broader US datasets.

Large‑scale analyses of NUFORC’s geocoded sightings across the entire United States show that state‑level clusters can emerge even after controlling for population and reporting biases; while these analyses are not specific to Maine’s internal pattern, they suggest that **geographic clusters can carry meaning beyond simple random distribution**.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nuforc.org/spatial/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nuforc.org">[NUFORC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nuforc.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Spatial Hotspots in NUFORC Reports – An Analysis &#124; NUFORCNovember 8, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 8, 2023</span></span></span>

Taken together, Maine’s map of UFO sightings shows a **consistent interstate pattern of reports**, a broad statewide geography of sightings, and annual fluctuations that reflect both genuine event reports and reporting practices — without requiring any single interpretive conclusion about the underlying causes.
