## Introduction

The Barrett Highway 59 story is one of the more intriguing late-reported UFO accounts connected to Wyoming. According to attorney Richard Barrett, he and his father, former Wyoming attorney general and federal appeals judge James Barrett, encountered a large illuminated object while driving between Gillette and Wright on Wyoming Highway 59 in April 1991. The account did not become widely known until decades later, which immediately creates a tension familiar in Wyoming UFO history: the witnesses appear serious and identifiable, yet the delay sharply limits what can be verified. Rather than settling the question of what happened, the case highlights how credible testimony can preserve a remarkable claim while still leaving investigators with major evidential gaps. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://cowboystatedaily.com/2026/05/16/35-years-after-ufo-encounter-[cheyenne" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cowboystatedaily.com">[Cowboy State Daily]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cowboystatedaily.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cowboy State Daily35 Years After UFO Encounter, Cheyenne Man Still Trying...May 16, 2026 — 16 May 2026 — Wyoming 59 between Gillette and...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 16, 2026</span></span></span>

## What the Barretts said happened on Wyoming 59

Richard Barrett's public account describes a late-night drive south from Gillette after a speaking engagement. He said that he and his father first noticed a bright light in the western sky around 11 p.m. What initially seemed like a star or distant light reportedly became more unusual as the journey continued. According to Barrett, the object appeared to shift position, grow brighter and eventually seem much closer to the vehicle. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://cowboystatedaily.com/2026/05/16/35-years-after-ufo-encounter-[cheyenne" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cowboystatedaily.com">[Cowboy State Daily]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cowboystatedaily.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cowboy State Daily35 Years After UFO Encounter, Cheyenne Man Still Trying...May 16, 2026 — 16 May 2026 — Wyoming 59 between Gillette and...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 16, 2026</span></span></span>

Barrett later described the object as enormous, silent and metallic in appearance. He said it displayed bright rotating lights and illuminated the surrounding area while showing darker sections that looked like windows or openings. The most dramatic part of the account is the claim that the object appeared to hover over or near the vehicle while they travelled along the highway. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://cowboystatedaily.com/2026/05/16/35-years-after-ufo-encounter-[cheyenne" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cowboystatedaily.com">[Cowboy State Daily]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cowboystatedaily.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cowboy State Daily35 Years After UFO Encounter, Cheyenne Man Still Trying...May 16, 2026 — 16 May 2026 — Wyoming 59 between Gillette and...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 16, 2026</span></span></span>

The setting matters. Highway 59 runs through sparsely populated country with long sightlines, limited ambient lighting and wide-open horizons. Those conditions can make distant lights appear unusually prominent and can complicate judgments of size, distance and motion, especially at night. That does not explain the Barrett account by itself, but it is one reason later investigators have difficulty treating a remembered visual impression as a precise measurement. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://cowboystatedaily.com/2026/05/16/35-years-after-ufo-encounter-[cheyenne" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cowboystatedaily.com">[Cowboy State Daily]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cowboystatedaily.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cowboy State Daily35 Years After UFO Encounter, Cheyenne Man Still Trying...May 16, 2026 — 16 May 2026 — Wyoming 59 between Gillette and...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 16, 2026</span></span></span>

A detail that gives the story some additional weight is Barrett's claim that he wrote down notes about the experience and conversations surrounding it shortly after the event. If such notes accurately survive from 1991, they would be more valuable than recollections recorded decades later because they would capture what the witnesses believed at the time rather than what memory reconstructed years afterwards. Public reporting on the case has referred to such contemporaneous notes, although they have not become part of a widely examined public evidential record. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://cowboystatedaily.com/2026/05/16/35-years-after-ufo-encounter-[cheyenne" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cowboystatedaily.com">[Cowboy State Daily]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cowboystatedaily.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cowboy State Daily35 Years After UFO Encounter, Cheyenne Man Still Trying...May 16, 2026 — 16 May 2026 — Wyoming 59 between Gillette and...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 16, 2026</span></span></span>

## What the named-witness record adds

The Barrett case attracts attention largely because neither witness was anonymous.

James Barrett was a prominent figure in Wyoming public life. He served as Wyoming attorney general before later becoming a judge on the United States Court of Appeals for the Tenth Circuit. Richard Barrett was likewise a practising attorney. Their identities are known, their careers are documented and neither man fits the stereotype of an anonymous internet storyteller appearing without any traceable background. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://cowboystatedaily.com/2026/05/16/35-years-after-ufo-encounter-[cheyenne" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cowboystatedaily.com">[Cowboy State Daily]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cowboystatedaily.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cowboy State Daily35 Years After UFO Encounter, Cheyenne Man Still Trying...May 16, 2026 — 16 May 2026 — Wyoming 59 between Gillette and...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 16, 2026</span></span></span>

That does not prove their interpretation was correct, but it changes the way the case is discussed. Many UFO reports rely on unnamed witnesses, second-hand retellings or stories that cannot be connected to identifiable people. The Barrett account avoids some of those problems. Readers know who the witnesses were, where they said the event occurred and approximately when they believed it happened. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://cowboystatedaily.com/2026/05/16/35-years-after-ufo-encounter-[cheyenne" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cowboystatedaily.com">[Cowboy State Daily]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cowboystatedaily.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cowboy State Daily35 Years After UFO Encounter, Cheyenne Man Still Trying...May 16, 2026 — 16 May 2026 — Wyoming 59 between Gillette and...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 16, 2026</span></span></span>

It may instead reflect the social stigma that many witnesses have historically associated with UFO reporting. ## Why the delay still leaves major gaps

The central weakness of the Barrett encounter is not necessarily the witnesses. It is time.

Because the story emerged publicly decades after the reported event, most of the evidence that investigators would normally seek was effectively gone. There are no publicly known photographs, no publicly known radar records connected to the event, no preserved police reports and no documented cluster of independent named witnesses who came forward at the time. By the time the account entered wider discussion, opportunities for immediate verification had long disappeared. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://cowboystatedaily.com/2026/05/16/35-years-after-ufo-encounter-[cheyenne" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cowboystatedaily.com">[Cowboy State Daily]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cowboystatedaily.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cowboy State Daily35 Years After UFO Encounter, Cheyenne Man Still Trying...May 16, 2026 — 16 May 2026 — Wyoming 59 between Gillette and...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 16, 2026</span></span></span>

The Barretts reportedly expected that other motorists might have reported the object and that local news coverage might appear the following day. According to Richard Barrett, they found no such reports. That absence does not prove nothing unusual occurred, but it removes one of the strongest forms of corroboration that often helps unusual sightings survive scrutiny. A giant illuminated object visible over a major roadway would ideally generate multiple independent reports. No such documented reporting wave is currently known. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://cowboystatedaily.com/2026/05/16/35-years-after-ufo-encounter-[cheyenne" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cowboystatedaily.com">[Cowboy State Daily]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cowboystatedaily.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cowboy State Daily35 Years After UFO Encounter, Cheyenne Man Still Trying...May 16, 2026 — 16 May 2026 — Wyoming 59 between Gillette and...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 16, 2026</span></span></span>

Memory also becomes a larger issue as decades pass. Psychological research has repeatedly shown that sincere witnesses can remember genuine experiences inaccurately in some details while remaining completely honest about believing the event occurred. Over long periods, perceptions of distance, duration, brightness and motion can become especially difficult to reconstruct precisely. The Barrett case therefore forces investigators to separate two questions:

* Did the witnesses genuinely experience something unusual?
* Did later descriptions accurately capture the object's size, position and behaviour?

Those are not necessarily the same question.

## Why witness credibility cannot replace evidence

The Barrett encounter is useful because it demonstrates a common misunderstanding in UFO debates. A credible witness and strong evidence are not identical things.

NASA's 2023 independent UAP study stressed that eyewitness reports may reveal patterns and point investigators towards events worth examining, but reports alone rarely provide enough information to reach firm conclusions. The study argued that reliable interpretation generally requires calibrated sensor data, multiple observations or other forms of corroboration.
