## Introduction

On **7 November 2006**, an unusual aerial event was reported over O'Hare International Airport when a group of United Airlines staff described seeing a **disc‑shaped object** hovering near **Gate C‑17** before it shot upwards through the cloud cover. Despite multiple eyewitnesses — including pilots, mechanics and ramp workers — the **U.S. Federal Aviation Administration (FAA)** did **not open a formal investigation** into the sighting. Instead, the agency characterised the event as a likely **weather phenomenon** rather than an unidentified or security‑critical incident. The FAA’s response and absence of a thorough inquiry remain central to how this case is discussed in UFO and aviation‑safety circles.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_O%27Hare_International_Airport_UFO_sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">2006 O&#x27;Hare International Airport UFO sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">2006 O&#x27;Hare International Airport UFO sighting</span></span></span>

## What the FAA Communications Confirmed

The official record of how the O’Hare sighting entered federal awareness comes primarily from **air‑traffic communications tapes** released in response to a **Freedom of Information Act (FOIA)** request by the *Chicago Tribune*. Before that request, FAA spokespeople publicly stated that the agency had **no information or investigation records** on the event.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-vault-files-2006-ohare-international-airport-ufo-sighting/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[The Black Vault]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the vault files 2006 ohare international airport ufo sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Black VaultThe Vault Files: 2006 O’Hare International Airport UFO Sighting - The Black Vault Case FilesOctober 28, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 28, 2025</span></span></span>

Once compelled by the FOIA process, the FAA reviewed tapes of communications from the afternoon of **7 November 2006** and **found a recorded call** in which a United Airlines operations supervisor phoned the FAA tower asking, in effect, whether controllers had seen “a flying disc” over **Gate C‑17**. These tapes show that airport personnel were discussing an unidentified object at the time, contradicting the FAA’s initial public denial of records.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-vault-files-2006-ohare-international-airport-ufo-sighting/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[The Black Vault]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the vault files 2006 ohare international airport ufo sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Black VaultThe Vault Files: 2006 O’Hare International Airport UFO Sighting - The Black Vault Case FilesOctober 28, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 28, 2025</span></span></span>

## Why No Formal Investigation Followed

FAA officials ultimately **declined to pursue a formal investigation**, basing their decision on several practical criteria within their operational remit:

* **No radar confirmation:** The object was not tracked by radar, a key tool for verifying airborne objects in controlled airspace.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_O%27Hare_International_Airport_UFO_sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">2006 O&#x27;Hare International Airport UFO sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">2006 O&#x27;Hare International Airport UFO sighting</span></span></span>
* **No interference with operations:** There was no evidence that the reported object disrupted **air traffic control procedures or aircraft safety** during the incident.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://rhino-lute-3zay.squarespace.com/skywatch-files/the-2006-ohare-airport-ufo-incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: rhino-lute-3zay.squarespace.com">[Things Visible &amp; Invisible]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">rhino-lute-3zay.squarespace.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the 2006 ohare airport ufo incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Things Visible &amp; InvisibleThe 2006 O’Hare UFO Sighting: How a Mysterious Encounter Changed Aviation Safety...</span></span></span>
* **Weather phenomenon explanation:** The agency’s publicly stated position attributed the sighting to a **meteorological effect**, most commonly a **hole‑punch cloud** (also called a fallstreak hole). This occurs when supercooled water droplets in a cloud suddenly freeze and fall out, creating a circular gap in the cloud deck — a visually striking but well‑understood natural phenomenon.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_O%27Hare_International_Airport_UFO_sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">2006 O&#x27;Hare International Airport UFO sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">2006 O&#x27;Hare International Airport UFO sighting</span></span></span>

FAA representatives stressed that since the reported object was neither tracked nor correlated with any flight hazard, it **did not meet internal thresholds for a UFO or unidentified aerial object inquiry**. In their view, routine radar checks and weather data sufficed to rule out an aviation safety threat.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://everything.explained.today/2006_O%27Hare_International_Airport_UFO_sighting/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: everything.explained.today">[Everything Explained]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">everything.explained.today</span><span class="citation-popover-title">2006 O&#x27;Hare International Airport UFO sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">2006 O&#x27;Hare International Airport UFO sighting explained...</span></span></span>

## How the Official Stance Shaped Public Perception

The FAA’s response — first claiming no records existed, then acknowledging limited communications but declining an investigation — coloured public and media perceptions of the O’Hare incident. The sequence raised questions about **transparency and protocol**:

* *Initial denials vs. FOIA revelations:* The contrast between early FAA statements and later FOIA‑revealed tape evidence led some commentators to argue that the agency **understated what it knew** at the time.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-vault-files-2006-ohare-international-airport-ufo-sighting/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[The Black Vault]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the vault files 2006 ohare international airport ufo sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Black VaultThe Vault Files: 2006 O’Hare International Airport UFO Sighting - The Black Vault Case FilesOctober 28, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 28, 2025</span></span></span>
* *Dismissal as weather vs. eyewitness accounts:* A formal “weather phenomenon” explanation satisfied many aviation and meteorological observers, but some witnesses — trained professionals familiar with aircraft and sky conditions — later expressed frustration that their observations were not formally investigated.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://simpleflying.com/chicago-o-hare-airport-reported-ufo-sighting-2006/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: simpleflying.com">[Simple Flying]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">simpleflying.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">chicago o hare airport reported ufo sighting 2006</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Simple FlyingDid You Know Chicago O&#x27;Hare Airport Had A Reported UFO Sighting In 2006?August 22, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 22, 2024</span></span></span>
* *Independent reporting:* Organisations like the **National Aviation Reporting Center on Anomalous Phenomena (NARCAP)** produced later independent reports analysing the event. Although these technical reviews did not lead to official re‑evaluation, they highlighted the event as an aviation safety anomaly worthy of discussion in professional contexts.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.wikidisc.org/wiki/2006_O%27Hare_International_Airport_Sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wikidisc.org">[WikiDisc]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wikidisc.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">2006 O&#x27;Hare International Airport Sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">2006 O&#x27;Hare International Airport SightingNovember 7, 2006...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 7, 2006</span></span></span>
