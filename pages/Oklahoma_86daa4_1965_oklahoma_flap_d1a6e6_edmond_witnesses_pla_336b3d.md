## Introduction

The Edmond phase of the 1965 Oklahoma UFO flap became important for a specific reason: it was one of the places where local witnesses openly challenged the Air Force's preferred explanation. During the first days of August 1965, reports of strange lights spread across central Oklahoma. Police officers, Highway Patrol personnel and ordinary residents described bright objects that appeared to hover, change colour and move in ways they did not expect from ordinary stars or planets. The Air Force response pointed towards astronomical objects, especially bright planets seen through atmospheric distortion. ## How Edmond officers and residents described the lights

By the time reports reached Edmond, the wider Oklahoma flap was already generating heavy attention. Sightings had been reported across southern and central parts of the state, and Highway Patrol channels were carrying numerous calls from officers and members of the public. Witnesses described bright lights showing multiple colours, including red, green, blue and white. Some accounts said the objects appeared to hover, while others reported sudden movement or changes in position.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.internationalufobureau.com/pastinvestigations/1965flap" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: internationalufobureau.com">[International UFO Bureau]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">internationalufobureau.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Descriptions of the flying objects varied.Read more</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">International UFO BureauThe 1965 UFO Sightings: A Historic Event InvestigatedThe flying objects were reported over Purcell, Norman, Chand...</span></span></span>

What made Edmond notable was not that the descriptions were completely unique. Rather, the reports arrived within an atmosphere of active observation. Police officers were already aware that similar sightings had been reported elsewhere in Oklahoma. Witnesses therefore compared what they were seeing with reports coming in from other locations rather than viewing the lights in isolation. Supporters of the UFO interpretation later argued that this wider pattern strengthened the credibility of the Edmond reports because multiple observers across different towns appeared to be describing related phenomena. Critics argued the opposite: once publicity spread, expectation and suggestion could influence how people interpreted bright objects in the night sky.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.internationalufobureau.com/pastinvestigations/1965flap" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: internationalufobureau.com">[International UFO Bureau]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">internationalufobureau.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Descriptions of the flying objects varied.Read more</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">International UFO BureauThe 1965 UFO Sightings: A Historic Event InvestigatedThe flying objects were reported over Purcell, Norman, Chand...</span></span></span>

The descriptions themselves sat at the centre of the dispute. Witnesses emphasised apparent motion, hovering behaviour and colour changes. The Air Force explanation focused on the way bright celestial objects can seem to flicker, shimmer and even appear to move when viewed near the horizon through unstable air. The question became whether observers were misreading astronomical effects or whether some reports involved something beyond a simple planetary sighting.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://edmondlifeandleisure.com/edmond-underground-in-edmond-abuzz-with-uco-sightings-p10350-87.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: edmondlifeandleisure.com">[edmondlifeandleisure.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">edmondlifeandleisure.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">edmond underground in edmond abuzz with uco sightings p10350 87</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">in &#x27;65 Edmond abuzz with UCO sightings11 Sept 2014 — The lights seen blinking and changing color were explained as a natural atmospheric...</span></span></span>

## What the Highway Patrol radio setting added to the story

One reason the Edmond reports remained memorable within Oklahoma UFO history was the role of Highway Patrol communications.

Later accounts from investigator Hayden C. Hewes described how television newsman Mike Buchanan alerted him after numerous UFO reports were being received by the Oklahoma Highway Patrol. Hewes reportedly travelled to a Highway Patrol communications tower near Edmond, where officers were monitoring incoming reports from around the region. According to these accounts, sightings were not isolated telephone calls arriving hours apart. Reports were allegedly being exchanged through official radio channels as events unfolded.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://alienexpanse.com/index.php?threads%2Fhighly-dubious-usaf-ufo-explanations.5093%2Fpost-109459=" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: alienexpanse.com">[Alien Expanse]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">alienexpanse.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Alien Expanse Highly Dubious USAF UFO Explanations</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Page 210 Dec 2020 —... highway patrol had received more than 20 reports of UFOs en route towards Oklahoma City. For believers, the radio traffic added credibility. The argument was straightforward: trained officers were reporting observations through professional communication networks rather than simply discussing rumours after the fact. The involvement of law-enforcement personnel gave the flap a degree of official visibility that many ordinary UFO stories never receive.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.internationalufobureau.com/pastinvestigations/1965flap" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: internationalufobureau.com">[International UFO Bureau]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">internationalufobureau.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Descriptions of the flying objects varied.Read more</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">International UFO BureauThe 1965 UFO Sightings: A Historic Event InvestigatedThe flying objects were reported over Purcell, Norman, Chand...</span></span></span>

The Edmond case remains useful precisely because both interpretations have plausible elements. The radio setting increased the seriousness of the reports, but it may also have increased the chances of collective misidentification.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://edmondlifeandleisure.com/edmond-underground-in-edmond-abuzz-with-uco-sightings-p10350-87.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: edmondlifeandleisure.com">[edmondlifeandleisure.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">edmondlifeandleisure.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">edmond underground in edmond abuzz with uco sightings p10350 87</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">in &#x27;65 Edmond abuzz with UCO sightings11 Sept 2014 — The lights seen blinking and changing color were explained as a natural atmospheric...</span></span></span>

## Why Robert Risser disputed the Air Force explanation

The most significant Edmond challenge came from Robert Risser, director of the Kirkpatrick Planetarium in Oklahoma City during the mid-1960s. Risser was not a UFO promoter. He was an astronomy educator whose professional work involved explaining the night sky to the public. That background gave his criticism unusual weight.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cdn.ymaws.com/www.ips-planetarium.org/resource/resmgr/planetarian/v16n3-July1987.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdn.ymaws.com">[YMAWS]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdn.ymaws.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">v16n3 July1987</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">THE PLANETARIAN3 Jul 1987 — Risser was Director of the. The Air Force explanation centred on bright celestial bodies, particularly Venus and other prominent objects visible during the period. Investigators argued that atmospheric scintillation — the same effect that causes stars to twinkle and shift colours — could account for many reports. Under certain viewing conditions, bright planets near the horizon can appear surprisingly dramatic, producing flashes of red, green and blue and seeming to drift or wobble. The Air Force considered this the most likely explanation for much of the Oklahoma wave.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://edmondlifeandleisure.com/edmond-underground-in-edmond-abuzz-with-uco-sightings-p10350-87.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: edmondlifeandleisure.com">[edmondlifeandleisure.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">edmondlifeandleisure.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">edmond underground in edmond abuzz with uco sightings p10350 87</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">in &#x27;65 Edmond abuzz with UCO sightings11 Sept 2014 — The lights seen blinking and changing color were explained as a natural atmospheric...</span></span></span>

Risser challenged that conclusion. According to later summaries of the case, he argued that some reported observations did not fit the proposed astronomical explanation. His criticism was important because it came from someone familiar with exactly the kinds of sky objects the Air Force was invoking. Rather than rejecting astronomy, he was arguing that astronomy alone did not adequately explain every report being discussed.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://edmondlifeandleisure.com/edmond-underground-in-edmond-abuzz-with-uco-sightings-p10350-87.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: edmondlifeandleisure.com">[edmondlifeandleisure.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">edmondlifeandleisure.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">edmond underground in edmond abuzz with uco sightings p10350 87</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">in &#x27;65 Edmond abuzz with UCO sightings11 Sept 2014 — The lights seen blinking and changing color were explained as a natural atmospheric...</span></span></span>
