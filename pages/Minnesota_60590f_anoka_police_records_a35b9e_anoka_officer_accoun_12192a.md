## Introduction

On the early morning of **19 February 2025**, a small group of **Anoka Police Department** personnel reported an extended sighting of an unusual aerial object. The episode has drawn attention because written witness statements and a short video clip were entered into the **U.S. National Archives’ Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena Records Collection** after being submitted to the **FBI**, giving rare public access to official‑format accounts of a modern, unresolved sighting. This page focuses strictly on *what the officers themselves described seeing* — the concrete observations from their testimony, without leaping to identification or wider interpretation.[FOX 9 Minneapolis-St. Paul]

## Timeline of the 19 February Sighting

Around **1.15–1.17 a.m. local time**, at least three Anoka police employees gathered in a parking area for an after‑action briefing following training when they noticed a lighted object on the **western horizon**. They continued watching the object over a period lasting **approximately ninety minutes**, during which time it changed position, altitude and appearance as described in their statements. At one point, they relocated to a nearby municipal ramp to get a clearer look.[FOX 9 Minneapolis-St. Paul]

One witness later recounted having seen a possibly related object on **17 February** during routine patrol, and records also referenced a **September 2022 sighting** near the Anoka Ice Arena, making the 19 February account part of a sequence of unusual reports from the area.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.aol.com/news/anoka-police-officers-reported-ufo-194940844.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aol.com">[AOL]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aol.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">anoka police officers reported ufo 194940844</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AOLAnoka police officers reported UFO sighting in early 2025, FBI filing says - AOL...</span></span></span>

## Descriptions of Colour, Shape and Movement

The officers’ accounts emphasise several recurring visual features of the object:

* **Shape and lighting:** Witnesses described the object as broadly **spherical**, with a pattern of **rapidly changing coloured lights** — red, blue, green and white — that gave it an appearance likened to “six *hula‑hoops with lights around each loop,” or a “tie‑dye” pattern. One described it as resembling an **orb with glowing loops** rather than a conventional aircraft lighting scheme.[FOX 9 Minneapolis-St. Paul]
* **Size estimate:** One observer guessed the object’s size to be roughly **comparable to a large vehicle**, with descriptions ranging from the heft of an SUV to about that of a **school bus** — estimates that, without external reference points, are highly approximate.[FOX 9 Minneapolis-St. Paul]
* **Light behaviour:** The multicoloured lights were said to flash and shift over time, and the witnesses explicitly noted that, while perhaps reminiscent of standard navigation lights, the object did *not* appear to match any known aircraft configuration.[FOX 9 Minneapolis-St. Paul]

Movement descriptions from the officers underscored perceived anomalies in how the object behaved:

* **Hovering and bursts:** Accounts said the object appeared to **hover**, drift and periodically shift location in “three separate bursts,” changing altitude and bearing over the course of the sighting.[FOX 9 Minneapolis-St. Paul]
* **Rapid motion:** One witness described a segment of motion as covering an estimated “**30–40 miles in a few seconds**,” with a movement style that “*skipped across the sky like a stone on a pond*.”[FOX 9 Minneapolis-St. Paul]
* **Directional reference:** Some statements placed the object’s later movement in the general direction of **Monticello**, and possibly near the **Monticello Nuclear Power Generating Plant**, although precise distances and bearings were not established by scientific tracking.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://hoodline.com/2026/05/cops-watch-bizarre-tie-dye-orb-near-monticello-nuke-plant/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hoodline.com">[Hoodline]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hoodline.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Anoka Police Multicolored Object In NARA UAP Files</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">HoodlineAnoka Police Multicolored Object In NARA UAP Files...</span></span></span>

## Observational Context and Recording Limitations

One of the officers attempted to document the sighting by using **binoculars paired with a smartphone camera**, but the resulting footage — a short, **blurry clip** — was released in the National Archives more as a witness artefact than clear evidence of specific characteristics. The officers noted that **cold weather** conditions hindered the use of more sophisticated optics like night vision equipment.[FOX 9 Minneapolis-St. Paul]

In an interview with a nonprofit that evaluates UAP reports, one officer estimated that the object may have been at an **altitude of 10,000–30,000 feet** and as far as **50 miles away** at times. Those kinds of ground‑based visual estimates are inherently uncertain without corroborating instruments, but the statement reflects the officers’ subjective sense of scale and distance.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.aol.com/news/anoka-police-officers-reported-ufo-194940844.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aol.com">[AOL]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aol.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">anoka police officers reported ufo 194940844</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AOLAnoka police officers reported UFO sighting in early 2025, FBI filing says - AOL...</span></span></span>

## What Witness Estimates Can and Cannot Prove

What the Anoka officers *reported* is rooted in subjective human observation: descriptions of colour, form and motion as perceived with the naked eye and basic optical aids. Their accounts constitute first‑hand testimony preserved in official U.S. archival records — a noteworthy data point within [Minnesota]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-minnesotas-skies/' | relative_url }})’s broader UFO/UAP history precisely because such detailed law‑enforcement testimony is uncommon in the public record.[FOX 9 Minneapolis-St. Paul]

However, several important limitations apply:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Size and distance estimates without instruments** are prone to error, especially at night without fixed reference points in the sky.
* **Movement interpretations** such as “30–40 miles in a few seconds” rely on implied assumptions about distance and cannot be verified without radar or multi‑sensor tracking.
* **Video quality**, as the officers themselves acknowledged, is insufficient to independently validate specific object characteristics.

</div>

Accordingly, while the officers’ descriptions are *what was reported*, they do not by themselves constitute positive identification of the object’s nature or origin — whether conventional, atmospheric, astronomical or something that genuinely fits within the UFO/UAP category in a technical sense. The federal record closed its review without reaching a conclusion, leaving the sighting unresolved in official terms.[FOX 9 Minneapolis-St. Paul]
