## Introduction

On the early morning of **19 February 2025**, a small group of **Anoka Police Department** personnel reported an extended sighting of an unusual aerial object. The episode has drawn attention because written witness statements and a short video clip were entered into the **U.S. National Archives’ Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena Records Collection** after being submitted to the **FBI**, giving rare public access to official‑format accounts of a modern, unresolved sighting. This page focuses strictly on *what the officers themselves described seeing* — the concrete observations from their testimony, without leaping to identification or wider interpretation.[FOX 9 Minneapolis-St. Paul]

## Timeline of the 19 February Sighting

Around **1.15–1.17 a.m. local time**, at least three Anoka police employees gathered in a parking area for an after‑action briefing following training when they noticed a lighted object on the **western horizon**. They continued watching the object over a period lasting **approximately ninety minutes**, during which time it changed position, altitude and appearance as described in their statements. At one point, they relocated to a nearby municipal ramp to get a clearer look.[FOX 9 Minneapolis-St. Paul]

One witness later recounted having seen a possibly related object on **17 February** during routine patrol, and records also referenced a **September 2022 sighting** near the Anoka Ice Arena, making the 19 February account part of a sequence of unusual reports from the area.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.aol.com/news/anoka-police-officers-reported-ufo-194940844.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aol.com">[AOL]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aol.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">anoka police officers reported ufo 194940844</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Anoka police officers reported UFO sighting in early 2025, FBI filing says - AOL...</span></span></span>

## Descriptions of Colour, Shape and Movement

The officers’ accounts emphasise several recurring visual features of the object:

* **Shape and lighting:** Witnesses described the object as broadly **spherical**, with a pattern of **rapidly changing coloured lights** — red, blue, green and white — that gave it an appearance likened to “six *hula‑hoops with lights around each loop,” or a “tie‑dye” pattern. One described it as resembling an **orb with glowing loops** rather than a conventional aircraft lighting scheme.[FOX 9 Minneapolis-St. Paul]
* **Size estimate:** One observer guessed the object’s size to be roughly **comparable to a large vehicle**, with descriptions ranging from the heft of an SUV to about that of a **school bus** — estimates that, without external reference points, are highly approximate.[FOX 9 Minneapolis-St. Paul]
* **Light behaviour:** The multicoloured lights were said to flash and shift over time, and the witnesses explicitly noted that, while perhaps reminiscent of standard navigation lights, the object did *not* appear to match any known aircraft configuration.[FOX 9 Minneapolis-St. Paul]

Movement descriptions from the officers underscored perceived anomalies in how the object behaved:

* **Hovering and bursts:** Accounts said the object appeared to **hover**, drift and periodically shift location in “three separate bursts,” changing altitude and bearing over the course of the sighting.[FOX 9 Minneapolis-St. Paul]
* **Rapid motion:** One witness described a segment of motion as covering an estimated “**30–40 miles in a few seconds**,” with a movement style that “*skipped across the sky like a stone on a pond*.”[FOX 9 Minneapolis-St. Paul]
* **Directional reference:** Some statements placed the object’s later movement in the general direction of **Monticello**, and possibly near the **Monticello Nuclear Power Generating Plant**, although precise distances and bearings were not established by scientific tracking.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://hoodline.com/2026/05/cops-watch-bizarre-tie-dye-orb-near-monticello-nuke-plant/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hoodline.com">[Hoodline]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hoodline.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Anoka Police Multicolored Object In NARA UAP Files</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Anoka Police Multicolored Object In NARA UAP Files...</span></span></span>

## Observational Context and Recording Limitations

One of the officers attempted to document the sighting by using **binoculars paired with a smartphone camera**, but the resulting footage — a short, **blurry clip** — was released in the National Archives more as a witness artefact than clear evidence of specific characteristics. The officers noted that **cold weather** conditions hindered the use of more sophisticated optics like night vision equipment.[FOX 9 Minneapolis-St. Paul]

In an interview with a nonprofit that evaluates UAP reports, one officer estimated that the object may have been at an **altitude of 10,000–30,000 feet** and as far as **50 miles away** at times. Those kinds of ground‑based visual estimates are inherently uncertain without corroborating instruments, but the statement reflects the officers’ subjective sense of scale and distance.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.aol.com/news/anoka-police-officers-reported-ufo-194940844.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aol.com">[AOL]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aol.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">anoka police officers reported ufo 194940844</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Anoka police officers reported UFO sighting in early 2025, FBI filing says - AOL...</span></span></span>

## What Witness Estimates Can and Cannot Prove

What the Anoka officers *reported* is rooted in subjective human observation: descriptions of colour, form and motion as perceived with the naked eye and basic optical aids. Their accounts constitute first‑hand testimony preserved in official U.S. archival records — a noteworthy data point within Minnesota’s broader UFO/UAP history precisely because such detailed law‑enforcement testimony is uncommon in the public record.[FOX 9 Minneapolis-St. Paul]

However, several important limitations apply:

<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Size and distance estimates without instruments** are prone to error, especially at night without fixed reference points in the sky.
* **Movement interpretations** such as “30–40 miles in a few seconds” rely on implied assumptions about distance and cannot be verified without radar or multi‑sensor tracking.
* **Video quality**, as the officers themselves acknowledged, is insufficient to independently validate specific object characteristics.

</div>

Accordingly, while the officers’ descriptions are *what was reported*, they do not by themselves constitute positive identification of the object’s nature or origin — whether conventional, atmospheric, astronomical or something that genuinely fits within the UFO/UAP category in a technical sense. The federal record closed its review without reaching a conclusion, leaving the sighting unresolved in official terms.[FOX 9 Minneapolis-St. Paul]

    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
