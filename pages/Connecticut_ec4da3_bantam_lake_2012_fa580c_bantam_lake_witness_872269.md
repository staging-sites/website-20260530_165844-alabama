## Introduction

On the early morning of **10 April 2012**, two independent witnesses in north‑western [Connecticut]({{ 'connecticut-ufos-from-colonial-legends/' | relative_url }}) reported seeing **a strange, glowing green object** descend from the night sky toward **Bantam Lake**. These accounts — from a passing motorist and a state police trooper — were widely reported by news agencies at the time and sparked curiosity across Connecticut and beyond. However, what observers actually saw and how authorities interpreted those reports remains rooted in a mix of eyewitness description, official reaction, and later sceptical analysis.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.police1.com//bizarre/articles/conn-trooper-motorist-strange-object-fell-from-sky-16LUPiOX605ty9CO/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: police1.com">[Police1]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">police1.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Conn. trooper, motorist: Strange object fell from sky</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Police1Conn. trooper, motorist: Strange object fell from skyApril 12, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 12, 2012</span></span></span>

## Motorist and Trooper Sightings

According to multiple news reports from April 2012, the first claim came from an **unidentified motorist driving near Litchfield at about 2 a.m.** who called state police to say they had seen a **“green, glowing object the size of a whale”** fall from the sky and disappear toward Bantam Lake. Around the same time, an **on‑duty Connecticut State Police trooper stationed about ten miles away in Warren** reported seeing something similar falling from the sky over the area of Bantam or nearby Morris. These two independent sightings on the same night were enough to attract immediate media attention.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.police1.com//bizarre/articles/conn-trooper-motorist-strange-object-fell-from-sky-16LUPiOX605ty9CO/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: police1.com">[Police1]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">police1.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Conn. trooper, motorist: Strange object fell from sky</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Police1Conn. trooper, motorist: Strange object fell from skyApril 12, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 12, 2012</span></span></span>

National UFO reporting databases captured these claims as well, noting the unusual nature of the description and the fact that witnesses described the object as **glowing and unusually large** — though the official report form indicated no concrete details about duration or shape beyond that. The witness to the NUFORC report chose to remain anonymous and did not provide contact information.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://nuforc.org/sighting/?id=88384" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nuforc.org">[NUFORC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nuforc.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NUFORCNUFORC UFO Sighting 88384April 18, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 18, 2012</span></span></span>

Local residents in the days following the incident also discussed seeing a **strange green glow** in the sky, and some said they *remembered* similar light phenomena in the same area in previous years. These anecdotal additions formed part of the informal oral history around the event, though they were not part of the official record.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.ctinsider.com/news/article/Litchifeld-Buzzing-With-Talk-of-UFO-Falling-Into-16892580.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ctinsider.com">[CT Insider]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ctinsider.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CT InsiderLitchifeld Buzzing With Talk of UFO Falling Into Bantam Lake; There Was a Meteor Shower That Night (UPDATED)April 11, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 11, 2012</span></span></span>

## Emergency Response and Lake Search

Following the initial reports, **Morris firefighters and crews from Bantam** were called to investigate, boating up and down portions of **Bantam Lake** in search of any sign of debris, a downed aircraft, or evidence of impact. Officials treated the situation initially as a possible **plane crash or aviation incident**, given the size and apparent descent described by witnesses. Despite several passes on the water in the early hours, **no wreckage, floating material, or physical evidence of any object was found**. After a thorough visual survey of accessible areas, authorities formally **called off the search** without locating anything.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.police1.com//bizarre/articles/conn-trooper-motorist-strange-object-fell-from-sky-16LUPiOX605ty9CO/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: police1.com">[Police1]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">police1.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Conn. trooper, motorist: Strange object fell from sky</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Police1Conn. trooper, motorist: Strange object fell from skyApril 12, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 12, 2012</span></span></span>

This lack of physical evidence was emphasised in press accounts — from the **Associated Press** to local Connecticut papers — and underscored by officials’ own statements that, beyond the eyewitness reports, there was nothing tangible to confirm an impact or object at the lake.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.ctinsider.com/news/article/Litchifeld-Buzzing-With-Talk-of-UFO-Falling-Into-16892580.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ctinsider.com">[CT Insider]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ctinsider.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CT InsiderLitchifeld Buzzing With Talk of UFO Falling Into Bantam Lake; There Was a Meteor Shower That Night (UPDATED)April 11, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 11, 2012</span></span></span>

## Limits of Evidence and Verification

One striking aspect of this sighting episode is the **absence of corroborating physical evidence**. No fragments, radar returns, aviation reports, or environmental anomalies were ever documented. The original motorist declined to stay at the scene or provide follow‑up contact information, and neither the trooper’s radio calls nor any detailed state police logs were publicly released in full. As a result, researchers such as the area‑based **Smoking Gun Research Agency** (which tracks unexplained phenomena) noted that while initial reports were intriguing, there was little to build a firm conclusion on beyond the eyewitness testimonies themselves.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.ctinsider.com/news/article/Litchifeld-Buzzing-With-Talk-of-UFO-Falling-Into-16892580.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ctinsider.com">[CT Insider]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ctinsider.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CT InsiderLitchifeld Buzzing With Talk of UFO Falling Into Bantam Lake; There Was a Meteor Shower That Night (UPDATED)April 11, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 11, 2012</span></span></span>

Authorities and local experts repeatedly pointed out that the **timing coincided with documented meteor activity**, which could provide a natural explanation for bright, fast‑moving light phenomena at night. In particular, the **National Weather Service** noted a meteor shower around that time, and observers have long noted that meteors — especially those containing iron or nickel — can appear green when burning in the atmosphere. Such meteors can also appear disproportionately large or close due to atmospheric perspective, leading multiple observers to report a vivid fall even when the object is high above the ground.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.ctinsider.com/news/article/Litchifeld-Buzzing-With-Talk-of-UFO-Falling-Into-16892580.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ctinsider.com">[CT Insider]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ctinsider.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CT InsiderLitchifeld Buzzing With Talk of UFO Falling Into Bantam Lake; There Was a Meteor Shower That Night (UPDATED)April 11, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 11, 2012</span></span></span>

In the absence of recovered material or scientific tracking data, sceptical explanations based on known atmospheric events remain the most widely accepted account among meteorologists and local officials. The combination of visual reports, a lack of physical evidence, and the known meteor shower context places the Bantam Lake sightings within the category of **unverified but mundane‑explainable observations** rather than confirmed anomalous objects.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://patch.com/connecticut/orange/investigation-continues-on-green-ufo-over-bantam-lake" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: patch.com">[patch.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">patch.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Investigation Continues on Green UFO over Bantam Lake | Orange, CT Patch</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">May 2, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2, 2012</span></span></span>
